# Team handbook

## Meetings
Daily standup is at 9:45 Pacific and lasts fifteen minutes. Backlog refinement is every second Wednesday.

## Code review
Reviews should get a first response within 24 hours. The payments-core repository needs two approvals; every other repository needs one.

## On-call
On-call rotates weekly on Mondays at 10:00. The secondary on-call is the person who was primary the week before.
